3. Conference Registration & Fees

Document conference dates, registration fees, and additional fees (e.g. abstract submission, poster printing, workshops). Additional fees populate "Other-Specify" on the TA form and itemize in the Travel Summary.

4. Flight Quotes & Comparisons

Document round-trip/one-way flights, search Google Flights, indicate comparison reasons and comparable pricing, and paste or upload comparison screenshots.
